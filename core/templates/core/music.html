Track and Field

By Sam Werlin

My Team

I am a part of the Hillhouse Track and Field team. We run at the Indoor Athletic Center at Hillhouse and the Outdoor Track nearby. I participate in meets during the season, and I train when we are between seasons. There are about 50 other students on my track team.

What I Run

At track, I mainly run the 1600 and the 3200, but I sometimes run the 4x800 relay. My personal record in the 1600 is 5:43, and my personal record in the 3200 is 12:46.

21st Century Skills

Throughout this year of running track, I have enhanced my 21st Century Skill: Initiative, Self-Direction and Accountability. By running with a team, I have taken responsibility for my own practice and I have gotten faster with the effort I have put in. I have also made goal times for myself to meet, and I have managed the amount of time in the season effectively in order to reach my goals. Except for when I take strategic breaks, I am constantly practicing so that I will be more ready for the next track meet.
